# Evidence Store架构

Evidence Store统一保存研究证据，而不是只保存“数据”。

包括：

- quantitative data
- qualitative documents
- policy documents
- case materials
- historical archives
- interviews
- papers
- news
- patents
- observations

每条证据应具备：

- source
- retrieval_time
- location
- provenance
- claim_links
- verification_status
- confidence
