# Paper Reading Prompt Set

## Skill extraction
Read using the validated paper-reading Skill. Extract only what the Skill specifies. Separate paper claims from inference and preserve evidence locations.

## Human-reading support
Do not summarize the paper. Based on my annotations/highlights, identify what is surprising, theoretically important, weak, underdeveloped or transferable.

## Adversarial dialogue
Attack the central argument. Identify hidden assumptions, rival explanations, evidence problems, measurement issues and scope conditions.

## Extension dialogue
Generate theoretical extensions, conceptual reframings, methodological transfers and research questions. Label each as evidence-based, plausible inference or speculation.

## Knowledge association
Compare with the existing vault. Generate candidate relationships by concept, theory, mechanism, evidence, method, contradiction, extension, reframing and project relevance. Do not write permanent knowledge.

## Research synthesis
Given the paper, my notes, AI dialogue and validated links, state what changed in my understanding. Separate what the paper establishes, what I infer, what remains uncertain and what it implies for current projects.

## Meta-reflection
Compare pre-reading assumptions with post-reading understanding. Identify which beliefs changed and what new questions emerged. Do not invent cognitive change.
