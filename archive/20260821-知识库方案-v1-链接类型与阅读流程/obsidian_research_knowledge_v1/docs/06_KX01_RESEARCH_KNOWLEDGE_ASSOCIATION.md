# KX01 Research Knowledge Association

## Purpose
Find meaningful intellectual connections between a paper and the existing Research Vault. This is not a simple similarity recommender.

## Search dimensions
1. Concept
2. Theory
3. Mechanism
4. Evidence
5. Method
6. Context
7. Contradiction
8. Extension
9. Reframing
10. Project relevance

## Procedure
1. Parse the paper into entities: concepts, theories, mechanisms, methods, claims, evidence and boundary conditions.
2. Search the vault.
3. Generate candidate relations.
4. For each relation produce relation type, target, textual/inferential basis, evidence and confidence.
5. Write to candidate area.
6. Promote only after review.

## Forbidden actions
- Do not label contradiction merely because findings differ.
- Do not claim theoretical extension without specifying the extension.
- Do not infer causal relations from topical similarity.
- Do not silently overwrite permanent knowledge.
