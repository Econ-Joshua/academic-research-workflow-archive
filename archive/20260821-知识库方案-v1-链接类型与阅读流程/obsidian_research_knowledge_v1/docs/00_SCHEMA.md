# Obsidian Research Knowledge System v1.0

## Core principle
The Obsidian vault is a research knowledge system, not a document archive. A paper is one node among Paper, Concept, Theory, Mechanism, Method, Evidence, Insight, Research Question, Project, and Research Memory.

## Four cognitive channels
1. Skill Extraction — structured, reusable extraction from the paper.
2. Human Reading — the researcher's own observations, judgments, doubts and ideas.
3. AI Dialogue — critique, extensions, rival explanations and reasoning generated through discussion.
4. Knowledge Association — links to prior papers, concepts, mechanisms, methods and projects.

These channels remain distinct and later converge through Research Synthesis.

## Permanent-knowledge rule
The permanent vault contains validated knowledge. AI-generated associations first enter a candidate area and require review before becoming permanent relational claims.

## Reading depth
- L1 Screening
- L2 Structured Reading
- L3 Deep Reading

L3 requires direct human reading, AI dialogue, cross-paper association, synthesis and meta-reflection.

## Human authority
Human authority is highest for research question, theoretical lens, mechanism commitment, contribution, evidence standard, research-design commitment, narrative architecture, writing taste and journal positioning.

## Link principle
Use relations for intellectual structure; tags for stable attributes.
