# Research Knowledge Object Rules

## Paper
Source-centered note.

## Concept
Reusable definitional object.

## Theory
Reusable explanatory framework.

## Mechanism
Process/causal logic comparable across studies.

## Method
Reusable research procedure/design.

## Evidence
Source-backed material supporting a claim.

## Insight
Nontrivial inference with research value.

## Research Question
Candidate question with potential research-program value.

## Project
Bounded research endeavor integrating knowledge objects.

## Research Memory
Cross-project durable understanding.

Promotion from paper notes to atomic objects requires durability, reuse or explicit human validation.
