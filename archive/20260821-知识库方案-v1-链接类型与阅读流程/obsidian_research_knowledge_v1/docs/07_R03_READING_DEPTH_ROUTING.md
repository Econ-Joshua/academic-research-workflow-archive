# R03 Reading Depth Routing

## L1 Screening
For uncertain/peripheral papers. Output relevance, priority and reason.

## L2 Structured Reading
For relevant papers that require reliable standard extraction.

## L3 Deep Reading
For foundational, theory-changing, design-changing or potentially transformative papers. Requires direct human reading, annotation, AI dialogue, association, synthesis and meta-reflection.

Route by expected cognitive impact, not journal prestige alone.
