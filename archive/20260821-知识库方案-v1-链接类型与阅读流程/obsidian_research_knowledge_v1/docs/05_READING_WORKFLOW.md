# Paper Reading Workflow

## Stage 0 — Triage
Use reading-depth routing.

## Stage 1 — Skill extraction
Extract standardized content while distinguishing paper claims from inference.

## Stage 2 — Human reading
Capture surprises, doubts, important passages, logic, ideas and personal judgments.

## Stage 3 — AI dialogue
Attack arguments, generate rival explanations, explore extensions, test assumptions and identify missing evidence.

## Stage 4 — Knowledge association
Compare with the vault across concepts, theories, mechanisms, evidence, methods, contradictions, extensions, reframings and project relevance. Store candidate links first.

## Stage 5 — Research synthesis
State what changed in understanding and implications for current projects.

## Stage 6 — Meta-reflection
Record durable cognitive updates.

## Stage 7 — Memory update
Promote only durable validated insights and relationships.
